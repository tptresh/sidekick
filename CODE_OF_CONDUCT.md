# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. This is a small hobby project; nobody here is owed
anything, and nobody deserves to be treated badly for asking a question or
getting something wrong.

## What is expected

- Assume the other person is acting in good faith.
- Criticise code and ideas, not people.
- Accept that "no" is a normal answer to a feature request, and that the
  maintainer may be slow.
- Respect that people work at different levels of experience.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of a sexual nature
- Publishing anyone's private information without permission
- Sustained disruption of discussions and reviews

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and
anywhere someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

Report a problem by opening a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/tptresh/spidey/security/advisories/new),
which is the only private channel this repo has, or by contacting the
maintainer through their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/tptresh).
Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

The maintainer will respond in a way that fits what happened: usually a private
word, sometimes editing or removing a comment, and for serious or repeated
problems a block from the repository. There is no appeals process; this project
has one maintainer, not a committee.

This document is a plain-language summary in the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
which is worth reading if you want the longer, more formal version.
